Frequently asked questions

        
Who is a property consultant in Sector 89, Gurgaon?+
MAAI Property Care is a real estate consultancy with its office at Shop 1 & 2, Proxima Commercial, Sector 89, Gurgaon, Haryana. We help people buy, rent, sell and manage property in Gurgaon. Call or WhatsApp +91 75894 90641.

        
How do I choose a good property consultant in Gurgaon?+
Look for a consultant with a local office you can visit, clear written fees and scope, and a habit of verifying title, approvals and the HRERA project record before you commit. Ask any consultant, including us, to explain these checks before you decide.

        
What services does MAAI Property Care offer?+
Buying, renting, selling and resale, letting out your property, and property management and care. We work with affordable homes, luxury residences, commercial spaces and plots.

        
Which areas do you serve?+
We serve Gurgaon (Gurugram), Haryana. Our office is in Sector 89 on the Dwarka Expressway side of the city.

        
Can you look after my Gurgaon property if I live in another city or abroad?+
Yes, property care for owners who live away is one of our services. Share the property location and what you need, and we will discuss the support we can provide.

        
Do you help with commercial property and plots?+
Yes. We help with commercial spaces and plots as well as homes. For plots, we recommend checking title, licence, approved layout and access before you pay.

        
How can I contact MAAI Property Care?+
Call or WhatsApp +91 75894 90641, email info@maaipropertycare.com, use the enquiry form on this page, or visit Shop 1 & 2, Proxima Commercial, Sector 89, Gurgaon.

        
Can I enquire about more than one service?+
Yes. Choose the main service you need and add any other requirements in your brief.

        
How are fees and service scope agreed?+
Your consultation should establish the required services, available support and fees. Review the proposal before deciding to proceed.

        
Can I discuss a property while living elsewhere?+
Include the property location and your current location in your brief so remote support and local availability can be discussed.
