About MAAI Property Care

Real estate consultancy in Sector 89, Gurgaon.

Who we are

MAAI Property Care is a real estate consultancy based in Sector 89, Gurgaon. Our promise is simple: Your Property, Our Responsibility. We aim to be one responsible partner for every property need, from the first question to the next move.

What we do

  • Buy: help finding and buying homes, plots and commercial property in Gurgaon.
  • Rent: help finding a home or commercial space to rent, and finding tenants for owners.
  • Sell / Resale: help positioning and selling your property.
  • Property management and care: tenant coordination, inspections, maintenance coordination and rent tracking, including for owners who live in another city or abroad.

We work across affordable homes, luxury residences, commercial spaces and plots.

How we work

  • We listen first and build a brief around your needs.
  • We explain scope and fees upfront, before you commit.
  • We encourage proper due diligence, including HARERA and document checks.
  • You get one clear point of contact.
