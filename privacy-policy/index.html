Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MAAI Property Care (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and protects information when you visit maaipropertycare.com or contact us. By using this website you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

MAAI Property Care is a real estate consultancy at Shop No. 1 & 2, Proxima Commercial Complex, Sector 89, Gurugram, Haryana 122505. HARERA Gurugram agent registration: RC/HARERA/GGM/4252/3847/2026/287. You can reach us at info@maaipropertycare.com or +91 75894 90641.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us: when you use our enquiry form or questionnaire, we collect the details you enter, such as your name, phone number or email, the service and property type you are interested in, your preferred location, budget and any message.
  • Messages: if you call, email or WhatsApp us, we receive your phone number or email address and the content of your messages.
  • Technical information: like most websites, our hosting provider and third-party services may automatically receive information such as your IP address, browser type, device, pages visited and the time of your visit.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and provide the property services you ask for
  • To arrange calls, site visits and follow-ups you request
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam or misuse
  • To meet legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

Enquiry form (FormSubmit)

Our enquiry form is delivered by email through FormSubmit, a third-party form service. When you submit the form, the details you enter are sent to FormSubmit’s servers and then forwarded to our email inbox. FormSubmit processes this data under its own privacy policy.

WhatsApp

If you choose to contact us on WhatsApp, or the form opens WhatsApp with your details pre-filled, your message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy and terms. We only see what you send us and use it to respond to you.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. We and our partners may use cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage) to:

  • remember your choices, for example that you have dismissed our cookie notice (stored in your browser’s local storage);
  • understand how visitors use the site (analytics);
  • show and measure advertising (see below).

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.

Google AdSense and third-party advertising

We may use Google AdSense to show advertisements on this website. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.

  • Google’s use of advertising cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, enables Google and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Other third-party ad vendors or networks may also use cookies to serve ads on our site. Their use of data is governed by their own privacy policies.

Analytics

We may use analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand, in aggregate, how visitors find and use our website. These tools may set cookies and collect information such as pages viewed, time on site, approximate location and device type. You can opt out of Google Analytics using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Embedded content

Some pages include embedded Google Maps. Embedded content from other websites behaves as if you visited that website and may collect data about you under that provider’s policy.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry details only as long as needed to respond to you, provide our services, and meet legal or regulatory requirements.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by emailing info@maaipropertycare.com. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 where it applies.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page with the date at the top.

Contact
