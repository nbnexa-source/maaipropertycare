# Design

- Palette: deep green `#132b25` / `#0f2620`, ivory `#f8f5eb`, gold `#dab675` (soft gold `#e5c990`), muted text `#d8ddd2`. WhatsApp green `#25d366` only for WhatsApp actions.
- Fonts: Manrope (headings, brand), DM Sans (body), Georgia italic for `<em>` accents in headings.
- Header: logo + "MAAI Property Care" + tagline, nav, pill "Let’s Talk ↗" CTA with gold border.
- Footer: brand, tagline, Explore links (Blog, About, Contact, Privacy, Terms, Disclaimer), address, phone, email, HARERA number.
- Blog: hero with floating logo orb and gold rule; cards with 16:9 cover, gold category chip, date and reading time; hover lift. Article: sticky table of contents on desktop, readable 740px column, gold callout notes, green WhatsApp CTA box.
- Motion: fade/slide-up reveals, gentle float on the hero orb; all disabled with `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Mobile: nav becomes a horizontal scroll strip under the logo; single-column cards; TOC above the article.
