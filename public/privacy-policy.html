Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how M/s. MAAI Property Care (“MAAI Property Care”, “we”, “us”), a real estate agent in Gurugram, Haryana, India, collects and uses personal information. We help clients with rentals, resale and new homes in Gurgaon.

1. Information we collect

When you send us an enquiry, we may collect your name, phone number, email address and your property needs (for example, the type of property, location, budget and timeline). You may share this with us through:

  • our website, maaipropertycare.com;
  • WhatsApp;
  • Facebook and Instagram;
  • YouTube.

2. How we use your information

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, contact you about it, and match you with suitable properties for rent, resale or new purchase.

3. We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone.

4. Third-party platforms

If you contact us through WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram (operated by Meta) or through Google services such as YouTube, those platforms also process your information under their own privacy policies and terms. We do not control how those platforms handle your data.

5. Google APIs and YouTube API Services

We use Google APIs, including YouTube API Services, to publish and manage our own content (such as property videos) on our own YouTube channel. By using or viewing that content, you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Access, correction and deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by emailing info@maaipropertycare.com. We will act on your request within a reasonable time, unless we are required by law to keep certain records.

7. Governing law

This policy is governed by the laws of India, including the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.
